About Me

Get to know me better

Hello! I'm Bishakha Prasad, a final-year B.Tech student in Electronics and Communication Engineering with a strong passion for software development. I specialize in developing secure, scalable, and user-centric applications using Java, Spring Boot, Spring Security, Hibernate, React, and MySQL, with a focus on building secure, scalable, and user-centric applications that solve real-world problems.

Through projects such as a Secure Online Banking System and a Full-Stack Generative AI Email Assistant, I have gained hands-on experience in backend and full-stack development, RESTful API development, authentication and authorization, database management, AI integration, and modern web technologies. These experiences have strengthened my understanding of software engineering principles and enhanced my ability to develop reliable, efficient, and maintainable applications.

Beyond project development, I actively strengthen my problem-solving skills by practicing Data Structures and Algorithms, enabling me to write clean, optimized, and efficient code. I am committed to continuous learning, exploring emerging technologies, and embracing new challenges. My goal is to contribute to innovative software solutions while growing as a Software Engineer in a collaborative and dynamic environment.
